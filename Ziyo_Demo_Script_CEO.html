Demo Script — Confidential

Ziyo App — CEO Presentation Demo

🎯 Audience: Ministry / Leadership
⏱ Demo: ~15 min
📱 Live prototype walkthrough
👤 Presenter: Pankaj
Goal of this demo: Show how Ziyo equips Uzbekistan's youth with the tools, knowledge, and opportunities they need to transition from education into meaningful careers — all in one app. Keep the focus on impact and outcomes, not features. Let the app speak visually; narrate the story of a student named Doston.

Before you start — setup checklist

  • Open Ziyo_App_Prototype.html in Chrome, full screen (F11)
  • Set browser zoom so the iPhone frame fills the screen comfortably
  • The app opens on the Home screen by default — confirm this before presenting
  • Keep this script open on a second screen or printed beside you
  • Silence notifications on your machine

1 — Opening Hook
No screen needed — speak directly to the room
~1 min
1
Set the context
"Uzbekistan has one of the youngest populations in Central Asia. Millions of young people are studying hard — but when they finish school or university, they struggle to find out: what opportunities exist? What skills do employers actually want? Where do I even start?

Ziyo answers those questions. Not as a website, not as a portal — but as a daily companion in their pocket."
👤
2 — Onboarding: Meet Doston
Show how a new user enters the platform
~1 min
2
Navigate to onboarding
SCREEN: Onboarding
Tap the Profile tab → tap "Profilni to'ldirish" button — or type go('onboarding') in console
"When Doston downloads Ziyo, the first thing it does is learn about him — his interests, his goals, the language he's most comfortable in. The platform is fully available in Uzbek, Russian and English."
Keep this brief — swipe through 2–3 onboarding cards only, then navigate to Home.
Tap the back arrow or navigate Home to move to the main experience
🏠
3 — Home: The Daily Experience
First impressions — content feed, QuickBytes
~2 min
3
Show the Home feed
SCREEN: Home
Tap the Home tab (house icon, bottom left)
"Every morning, Doston opens Ziyo and sees what's relevant for him today — upcoming events, new grants, job opportunities, and short learning content. Everything personalised, nothing buried."
Slowly scroll the home feed to show the different content cards — events, jobs, coins balance.
4
Show QuickBytes — bite-sized learning
SCREEN: QuickBytes (v-bytes)
Scroll home feed up to the QuickBytes rail → tap any video thumbnail card
"QuickBytes are 60-second learning moments — career tips, skill micro-lessons, real advice from practitioners. Short enough to watch on a commute, specific enough to actually be useful. Doston just learned something new in under a minute."
Swipe between the 3 bytes (Asilbek, Doston, Kamola team videos) to show the vertical feed format.
Tap the back arrow to return to Home, then tap the Events section
🎯
4 — Opportunity Discovery
Events · Grants · Jobs & Internships
~3 min
5
Events
SCREEN: Events (v-events)
Tap "Tadbirlar" / Events from the home feed or navigation
"All career events, bootcamps, and workshops are aggregated here. Doston can see the IT Career Day coming up — he taps to get the full details, registers in two taps, and gets a digital ticket."
Tap an event card to show the event detail screen. Show the Register button and the Add to Calendar / Set Reminder options.
6
Grants & Scholarships
SCREEN: Grants (v-grants)
Back → tap Grants from the home feed or go('grants')
"Grants and scholarships from government bodies, international programmes, and partners — all in one place, with deadline tracking and filters for country, type, and duration. Before Ziyo, a student would miss these because they were scattered across different websites."
Use the filter chips at the top to show filtering in action. Tap one grant to show the detail view.
7
Jobs & Internships
SCREEN: Jobs (v-jobs)
Back → tap Jobs from the home feed or go('jobs')
"Entry-level jobs, internships, and project-based work — specifically curated for young people. Doston can filter by salary range, duration, and type. When he finds something, he applies directly through the app — and every application is tracked toward his ZiyoCoins."
Tap a job listing to show the job detail. Highlight the Apply button and the bookmark/save feature.
Key message: "Three types of opportunity — events, funding, work — unified in one place. Young people in Uzbekistan no longer need to know the right website or the right person. Ziyo surfaces the opportunity for them."
📚
5 — Learning & Skill Building
Courses · Podcasts
~2 min
8
Courses
SCREEN: Courses (v-courses)
Back → tap Courses from the home feed or navigation
"Structured learning paths — from digital skills to soft skills and professional certifications. Doston can browse, enrol, and track his progress. Courses are sourced from trusted partners and vetted for quality."
Tap a course card to show the course detail — highlight the rating stars, enrol button, and lesson structure.
9
Podcasts
SCREEN: Podcasts (v-podcasts)
Back → tap Podcasts from the home feed
"For learners who prefer audio — career interviews, industry insights, language practice. They can listen while commuting, working, or studying. Every episode can be bookmarked to revisit later."
Tap a podcast to show the player. Quick — 20 seconds here is enough.
🏆
6 — Engagement & Motivation
ZiyoCoins · Leaderboard · Shop
~2 min
10
ZiyoCoins — gamified engagement
SCREEN: Profile (coin section visible)
Tap the Profile tab (person icon, bottom right) → scroll to the ZiyoCoins Wallet card
"Everything Doston does on the platform earns him ZiyoCoins — completing a course, attending an event, applying for a grant, referring a friend. He has earned 1,260 coins purely through his active engagement with the platform."
11
Leaderboard — healthy competition
SCREEN: Leaderboard (v-leaderboard)
Scroll down on profile to the leaderboard promo card → tap it
"Young people are naturally competitive. The leaderboard makes engagement visible — Doston is currently ranked 3rd, 290 coins behind the leader. That gap is motivating, not daunting. Switch to the Weekly tab to show how it resets and re-energises each week."
Tap Weekly, then All Time tabs to demonstrate dynamic ranking and coin amounts updating.
12
ZiyoCoins Shop — real-world rewards
SCREEN: Shop (v-shop)
Tap back → tap "🎁 Redeem your ZiyoCoins" button on the wallet card
"Coins aren't just points — they're redeemable for real items in the shop. Earphones, books, merchandise. This closes the loop: learning and engagement lead to tangible rewards. It makes staying active on Ziyo genuinely worthwhile."
Key message: "The gamification layer isn't decoration. It's a retention engine — turning passive users into habitual learners by rewarding every positive action they take on their career journey."
📋
7 — Personal Growth Tracking
Profile · Skills & CV
~2 min
13
Profile — the full picture
SCREEN: Profile (v-profile)
Tap the Profile tab
"Doston's profile is his career identity inside Ziyo — his completion rate, his earned coins, the events he's attended, the courses he's finished. It grows with him. Employers and mentors can see this as a signal of drive and intent, not just grades."
Scroll slowly through the profile — highlight the completion bar, the activity stats, the leaderboard rank, and the bookmarks.
14
Skills & CV
SCREEN: Skills & CV (v-skills)
Tap the "Skills & CV" card on the profile page
"Doston can tag his skills directly in the app and attach his CV. The AI analyses his skill profile against the jobs and opportunities on the platform and tells him exactly what to learn next to become a stronger candidate. The gap between 'I'm studying' and 'I'm ready to work' becomes visible — and actionable."
Tap the + button to show the skill-add flow. Point out the AI analysis card at the bottom.
🌟
8 — Closing: The Vision
No screen needed — speak directly
~1 min
15
Land the key message
"What you've just seen is Doston's full journey — from a student who doesn't know where to start, to someone who is actively building his career, tracking his skills, attending the right events, applying for the right opportunities, and staying motivated every step of the way.

Ziyo isn't just an app. It's a career infrastructure for an entire generation. And this is just the beginning."
16
Open for questions
"I'd be happy to dive deeper into any screen, walk through how specific metrics are tracked, or show any other part of the experience. What would you like to explore?"
Have the prototype still visible. If asked about data/metrics, pivot to the KPI Dashboard.
