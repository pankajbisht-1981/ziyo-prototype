How to set up in Telegram — step by step
  1. Open your Ziyo Forum Telegram channel in the admin view
  2. Tap New Post → Poll for Q1–Q7. Copy each question and options exactly as shown below. Set multi-choice polls (🔲) to "Multiple answers" mode.
  3. For Q8 (open text): post as a regular message and ask users to reply in the comments, or link to a Google Form for the open-text field.
  4. Post the announcement message first, then the 8 polls one by one. Pin the announcement to the channel.
  5. Polls can stay open for 7 days. After closing, export results from Telegram and share with the SAP team.
