Ziyo Forum — Organisation Background
  • Founded 2019 with 2 people; now 100+ employees, growing year on year
  • Status: NGO — mission to bridge students and government/ministries
  • Partners include the UN and multiple international organisations
  • University partnerships: Les Roches, Lucerne, Ducas; Russian and now Chinese universities
  • Programmes: student exchange abroad, student initiative support, grants for startups
Ministry Consolidation — Just Happened (4 Oct 2026)
Uzbekistan previously had three separate ministries: Ministry of Preschool Education, Ministry of School Education, and Ministry of Higher Education. Two days before this meeting, all three were merged into a single unified Ministry of Education. This is a significant policy shift — Ziyo Forum's role as a bridge between students and the government now points to a single counterpart, which could simplify partnership and advocacy work considerably.
Education Gap — Core Problem
1.6 million students graduate from high school in Uzbekistan each year. Only 600,000 go on to university — universities have capacity for just 425,000 places. The remaining ~1 million receive no structured further education. Ziyo Forum's mission is to reach this underserved majority with free, motivating educational content and opportunities.
Ziyo App — Vision & Intended Direction

Original intent (Bobur):

  • Aggregate all Ziyo Forum offerings in one place and track what students engage with
  • Motivate participation through Zeocoin gamification — reward progress
  • Suggest personalised trajectories based on interests (recommendation engine)
  • Simplify registration — one app instead of multiple separate sign-ups

Key differentiator:

No single education platform exists in Uzbekistan. Opportunities are scattered across Telegram channels, social media, and university pages. Ziyo aims to be the distraction-free, focused alternative to social media noise.

Expansion roadmap (priority order):

1
Events — current focus; Ziyo Forum's own events first, then partner organisations
2
Grants, scholarships & news — consolidated education-relevant information
3
Community — student clubs, group projects, peer networking within the app
4
Gamification expansion — achievement layers to drive motivation and progression
5
Media content — educational media in one trusted place
Critical Issues Identified
Issue Severity Detail
No single product vision Critical IT team, working team, management, and users all hold different mental models of what the app should be. The product pivots toward whoever spoke last. Bobur acknowledged there is no concrete, agreed-upon end state that everyone can execute against.
App still in demo state Critical Bobur explicitly described the app as a "demo version" — not ready for serious marketing. No marketing campaigns have been run. The lack of a defined vision is the direct cause; the product cannot be marketed until the direction is locked.
No in-house development team HIGH All development is outsourced. Outsource was chosen precisely because the vision was not finalised. Once the direction is clear, Ziyo intends to hire an internal development team — the resources exist, but the trigger is vision clarity.
Bobur's Explicit Ask to SAP Team
"Provide your personal and international experience on what the final version should look like — then we can take it in our own hands and work on it correctly. We need that one concrete vision. That's why we haven't been able to work on it correctly."
Growth Drivers & Scaling Plan

Bobur's priority order for growth:

  1. Fix the app and the content — nothing else works without this
  2. Expand partnerships to other cities and organisations (Tashkent-centric today)
  3. Marketing + showcase real student success stories through the app

Scaling plan:

  • Currently outsourced; once vision is finalised, invest significantly and hire in-house team
  • Resources to scale are available — the constraint is clarity, not funding
  • Long-term ambition: grow to national scale — "the way Facebook started as a student network"
  • Bobur cited Uzbekistan has no equivalent platform; Ziyo wants to be first
